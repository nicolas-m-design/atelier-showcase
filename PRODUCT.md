# Atelier showcase

This public showcase helps portfolio visitors understand how Atelier reviews working product screens. Its main task is to let someone watch canvas navigation, entry into a real preview, a clear product interaction and the return to the canvas.

The recording uses Marvin’s fictional fleet and synthetic operational data. It shows the real local applications; it does not claim measured user outcomes or a publicly available Atelier application.

## Demo player scope

The static GitHub Pages player exists because GitHub’s normal MP4 file page offers a download instead of inline playback. Keep native playback controls, a matching poster, a direct download, a transcript and a link back to the repository. It requires no account, scripts, analytics or external embed.

Inherit Atelier’s quiet light canvas and Inter typography. The video leads; the surrounding interface stays small. This extension does not introduce a new visual system or product features. On narrow screens the player scales to fit and its links wrap; fullscreen and download remain available for detailed viewing.

## Publication boundary

Publish only approved showcase documentation, reviewed media, the static player and its licensed font. Application source, project registries, machine paths, raw source/build identity and unreviewed captures stay private. The README’s existing screenshots and explanations remain useful for close inspection.

## Quality bar

A first-time visitor can find Play immediately, understand the canvas/interaction boundary from the sequence, and reach the ending without a broken media request. Preserve real app timing and disclose composed captions/cursor treatment. Verify the public player, download and transcript after publishing. Scope is demonstration and viewing, not a landing-page redesign.
